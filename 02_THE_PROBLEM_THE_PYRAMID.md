# The Problem: The Current System (The Pyramid)

## What Is The Pyramid?

**The Pyramid is a structure of control, not truth.**

It operates on these principles:

```
Power flows UP (to the top)
Wealth flows UP (to the elite)
Control flows DOWN (to the masses)
Truth is managed (not spoken)
```

---

## The Three Lies That Hold The Pyramid

### Lie 1: Centralization Is Efficient

**The claim:**
"Power concentrated at the top makes better decisions faster."

**The reality:**
```
Centralized decisions are made by people far from problems.
Far from consequences.
Far from truth.

Result: Decisions are terrible, but they serve the center, not the people.
```

### Lie 2: Scarcity Is Real

**The claim:**
"There is not enough. We must control distribution."

**The reality:**
```
Scarcity is manufactured to maintain control.
Land, water, food, energy - all abundant.
But controlled to keep prices high and power concentrated.

Result: Artificial scarcity creates artificial hierarchy.
```

### Lie 3: You Cannot Be Trusted

**The claim:**
"People are selfish and must be controlled for their own good."

**The reality:**
```
When people have their needs met (food, water, safety, meaning):
They are generous.
They are creative.
They are wise.

When their needs are denied:
They become selfish and desperate.

Result: The system creates the behavior it claims to prevent.
```
